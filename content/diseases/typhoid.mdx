## What is Typhoid Fever?

Typhoid fever is a serious bacterial infection caused by Salmonella Typhi. It spreads through food or water contaminated with the bacteria, usually linked to poor sanitation or hygiene. The infection affects the whole body, causing a sustained high fever along with gastrointestinal and other systemic symptoms. Typhoid remains a significant public health concern in many parts of the world, including some regions of India, and requires prompt diagnosis and antibiotic treatment because it can become life-threatening if not properly managed.

## Symptoms

Typhoid fever typically develops gradually, over one to three weeks after exposure, and may include:

- Sustained high fever that can rise in a stepwise pattern over several days
- Headache and generalised body aches
- Weakness, fatigue, and loss of appetite
- Abdominal pain and discomfort
- Either constipation or diarrhoea, which can vary during the illness
- A rash of flat, rose-coloured spots in some cases
- An enlarged liver or spleen, which a doctor may detect on examination

Without treatment, symptoms can worsen progressively over several weeks and lead to serious complications.

## Causes & Risk Factors

Typhoid fever is caused by infection with Salmonella Typhi bacteria, which are usually acquired through:

- Drinking water contaminated with sewage or the bacteria
- Eating food prepared or handled by someone infected with the bacteria, including asymptomatic carriers
- Eating raw fruits or vegetables washed with contaminated water
- Poor sanitation infrastructure in the local area

Risk is higher in areas with inadequate access to clean water and sanitation, during travel to such regions without proper precautions, and in close contact with someone who has typhoid or is a carrier of the bacteria.

## Homeopathy and Conventional Treatment

Conventional treatment for typhoid fever relies on a course of antibiotics prescribed by a doctor, chosen based on local resistance patterns and the severity of illness, along with supportive care such as fluids and rest. Hospitalisation may be needed for more severe cases, particularly where complications or dehydration are present. Antibiotic treatment is essential; delayed or inadequate treatment significantly raises the risk of serious complications, including intestinal perforation and spread of infection through the bloodstream.

Homeopathy should never be used as an alternative to antibiotics in typhoid fever. Continue your current treatment and consult your doctor before making changes, and only consider complementary approaches, if at all, as an addition alongside medically supervised antibiotic treatment.

## Lifestyle & Self-care Tips

- Drink only safe, boiled, filtered, or bottled water during and after recovery
- Rest adequately and stay well hydrated with oral rehydration solutions if advised
- Eat soft, easily digestible, hygienically prepared food as appetite allows
- Practice thorough hand hygiene, especially before eating and after using the toilet
- Avoid preparing food for others until cleared by your doctor, to prevent spreading infection
- Complete the full prescribed course of antibiotics even if you start to feel better
- Attend follow-up appointments to confirm the infection has cleared

## When to See a Doctor Urgently

Typhoid fever can become life-threatening without proper treatment. Seek emergency medical care immediately if you or someone else has:

- High fever that persists or keeps rising despite treatment
- Severe abdominal pain, swelling, or a rigid abdomen, which can suggest intestinal perforation, a medical emergency
- Confusion, severe drowsiness, or difficulty staying alert
- Signs of severe dehydration, such as very reduced urination, dizziness, or sunken eyes
- Blood in stool or vomiting blood
- Any rapid worsening of symptoms during illness or treatment

Because untreated or inadequately treated typhoid can be life-threatening, these symptoms need emergency medical attention without delay, and homeopathy must never be used as a substitute for this urgent antibiotic care.
