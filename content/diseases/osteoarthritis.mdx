## What is Osteoarthritis?

Osteoarthritis is a long-term joint condition in which the smooth cartilage that cushions the ends of bones gradually wears down, causing the joint to become painful, stiff, and less mobile over time. It is the most common form of arthritis and most frequently affects the knees, hips, hands, and spine, though it can occur in any joint. As cartilage thins, the bones underneath can rub against each other, sometimes leading to small bony growths at the joint margins.

## Symptoms

Common symptoms of osteoarthritis include:

- Joint pain that tends to worsen with activity and ease with rest
- Stiffness, especially after waking up or after periods of inactivity
- Swelling or tenderness around the affected joint
- A grating, crackling, or clicking sensation (crepitus) during joint movement
- Reduced range of motion in the joint
- A feeling of joint instability or weakness in some cases

Symptoms can range from mild and occasional to persistent and disabling, and they often develop gradually over months or years.

## Causes & Risk Factors

The exact cause of osteoarthritis is not fully understood, but it results from a combination of mechanical wear and biological changes in the joint. Several factors are known to increase the risk, including:

- Increasing age
- Being female, particularly after menopause
- Excess body weight, which adds strain to weight-bearing joints like the knees and hips
- Previous joint injury or surgery
- Repetitive stress on a joint from certain occupations or high-impact activities
- Joint misalignment or abnormal joint shape
- A family history of osteoarthritis
- Other joint conditions, such as rheumatoid arthritis or gout, which can lead to secondary osteoarthritis

## Homeopathy and Conventional Treatment

Conventional management of osteoarthritis typically includes weight management, regular low-impact exercise, physiotherapy, supportive devices such as braces or walking aids, pain-relief medication, and in more advanced cases, joint injections or surgery such as joint replacement.

Some patients choose to use homeopathy as a complementary approach alongside these treatments, often seeking general support alongside their prescribed care. Homeopathy should not be seen as a replacement for medically recommended osteoarthritis treatment. Continue your current treatment and consult your doctor before making changes.

## Lifestyle & Self-care Tips

- Stay as active as comfortably possible with low-impact exercise such as walking, swimming, or cycling
- Work towards and maintain a healthy body weight to reduce strain on weight-bearing joints
- Wear supportive, well-fitting footwear
- Use joint-protective aids such as braces or walking sticks if recommended by your doctor or physiotherapist
- Apply heat or cold packs to ease pain and stiffness
- Pace daily activities and avoid prolonged repetitive strain on affected joints
- Build muscle strength around affected joints through guided exercise

## When to See a Doctor Urgently

Most osteoarthritis symptoms develop and are managed gradually, but seek prompt medical attention if you experience:

- Sudden, severe joint pain or swelling that comes on quickly
- A joint that becomes hot, red, and very tender, which may suggest infection or another condition rather than simple osteoarthritis
- Fever accompanying joint pain and swelling
- Sudden inability to move or bear weight on a joint
- Joint pain following a significant injury or fall

If you have persistent joint pain, stiffness, or swelling that is not improving, speak to your doctor for an accurate diagnosis and appropriate care plan.
