## What is Chronic Constipation?

Chronic constipation is a long-standing pattern of infrequent, hard, or difficult-to-pass bowel movements that lasts for weeks or longer, rather than being a short, one-off episode. It can affect people of any age, though it becomes more common with increasing age, and is generally very manageable through dietary and lifestyle changes, though some cases need further medical evaluation.

## Symptoms

Common symptoms of chronic constipation include:

- Having fewer than three bowel movements a week
- Hard or lumpy stools that are difficult to pass
- Straining during bowel movements
- A sensation of incomplete emptying after passing stool
- Bloating, stomach ache, or discomfort
- Occasional nausea

## Causes & Risk Factors

Common causes and contributing factors include:

- A diet low in fibre
- Not drinking enough fluids
- Low levels of physical activity or a sedentary lifestyle
- Regularly ignoring the urge to pass stool
- Changes in routine, such as travel
- Certain medications, including some painkillers (such as opioids), iron supplements, and other drugs
- Stress or anxiety
- Underlying conditions affecting the muscles, nerves, or hormones involved in bowel movements, such as irritable bowel syndrome, diabetes, an underactive thyroid, or certain neurological conditions
- Pregnancy and increasing age are also recognised risk factors

## Homeopathy and Conventional Treatment

Conventional management of chronic constipation usually starts with dietary and lifestyle changes, such as increasing fibre and fluid intake, regular physical activity, and establishing a consistent toilet routine. A pharmacist or doctor may recommend over-the-counter laxatives for short-term use, most of which work within about three days. If constipation persists despite these measures, a doctor may investigate for an underlying cause and suggest further management.

Some patients choose to use homeopathy as a complementary approach alongside this care, often seeking general digestive support alongside their prescribed treatment. Homeopathy should not be used as a replacement for prescribed laxatives or investigation of an underlying cause. Continue your current treatment and consult your doctor before making changes.

## Lifestyle & Self-care Tips

- Increase dietary fibre gradually, including fruits, vegetables, and whole grains
- Drink plenty of water throughout the day
- Stay physically active with regular movement or exercise
- Try to respond promptly to the urge to pass stool rather than delaying
- Establish a regular toilet routine, allowing enough unhurried time
- Avoid excessive straining, which can contribute to piles or fissures
- Limit long-term reliance on laxatives without medical guidance

## When to See a Doctor Urgently

See your doctor if constipation persists despite dietary and lifestyle changes or over-the-counter treatment, occurs regularly, or represents a new and unexplained change in your usual bowel pattern. Seek prompt medical attention if you notice:

- Blood in the stool or on toilet paper
- Unexplained weight loss
- Persistent abdominal pain, swelling, or a feeling of a lump in the abdomen
- Severe, worsening pain or inability to pass stool or gas at all
- Ongoing fatigue alongside bowel changes

These symptoms need timely medical evaluation to identify or rule out an underlying cause.
